# Getting started

Hesabak opens with a fictional sample (Layla Mansour billing Nile Bakery Co.) so you can see a finished invoice straight away. Nothing you do with the sample is sent anywhere.

## Your first invoice

1. Open [hesabak.hatemsoliman.dev](https://hesabak.hatemsoliman.dev/app/).
2. Press **Set up my profile** on the yellow banner. Fill in your name, role, email and address on the **Profiles** tab. The page on the right updates as you type.
3. On **Design**, type your wordmark (for example `yourname` and `.studio`) and pick an accent colour.
4. Back on **Details**, fill in the client, a client code such as `ACME`, and press **Next** to get the number `ACME-2026-01`.
5. Choose how you bill: **Day rate from hours** or **Line items**. See [[Billing and Tax]].
6. Press **Save** to keep it, then **Save PDF** to print it.

## The four tabs

| Tab | What lives there |
|---|---|
| **Details** | The document itself: client, number, dates, billing, tax, payments, notes |
| **Design** | Template, colours, block order, wordmark, tagline and footer |
| **Profiles** | Who you bill as, and your saved clients |
| **Saved** | Every saved invoice and receipt, export, import, and erase |

## The buttons along the top

| Button | What it does |
|---|---|
| **New** | Starts a fresh invoice for the same client with the next number |
| **Duplicate** | Copies this invoice to a new number and today's date, clearing payments |
| **Make receipt** | Saves the invoice and turns it into its receipt (see [[Receipts]]) |
| **Save** | Stores the current document in this browser |
| **Save PDF** | Opens the print dialog with the file named after the invoice number |
