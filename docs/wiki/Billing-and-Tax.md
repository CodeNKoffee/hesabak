# Billing and tax

## Day rate from hours

Enter the hours you worked, your hours per day (7 by default) and your day rate. Hesabak divides and **rounds down to a tenth of a day**, so the client never pays for part of a day you didn't work.

> 40 hours ÷ 7 hours per day = 5.71, rounded down to **5.7 days**. At EGP 2,000 a day, that's **EGP 11,400**.

Add a **time record** such as "Toggl export, 1–12 March" so the client can match it to your log.

## Line items

Switch to **Line items** for fixed prices: a description, quantity and unit price per row. **Add item** adds a row; the × removes one.

## Advance already received

If the client paid part up front, enter it under **Payments → Advance already received**. The invoice shows "Less: advance already received" and a **Balance due** for the rest.

## Tax

| Setting | Use it when |
|---|---|
| **No tax** | You aren't registered for VAT (the default) |
| **Egypt VAT 14%** | You charge Egyptian VAT |
| **Custom** | Any other tax: set your own label and rate |

Tick **Prices already include tax** if your price is tax-inclusive (Hesabak works the tax out of it). Leave it unticked to add tax on top.

## Currencies

EGP, USD, EUR and AED, with thousands separators (EGP 11,400). Each profile has a default currency.

## Due date and payment details

Set a **Due date** so the client knows when to pay. Put how to pay in the **Payment** note, and optionally a **Payment link** (an InstaPay or payment page) that shows as "Pay online" in the Standard template.
